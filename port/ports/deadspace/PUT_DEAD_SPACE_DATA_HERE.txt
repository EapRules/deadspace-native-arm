Put your own Dead Space Mobile Xperia Play v1.1.33 APK, ZIP, or extracted
folder in this directory. The filename does not matter.

Dead Space PortMaster port and eapx extractor by EapRules.

The port validates this exact native library before extracting anything:

  lib/armeabi/libEAMGameDeadSpace.so
  SHA1 0ed42b611415015807f759ec9b5457857143ce39

Known labels for the supported data include:

  Dead Space Mobile Version 1.1.33
  Dead Space Mobile for Xperia Play v1.1.33
  dead space vita 1.1.33

The known Vita-ready deadspace.zip is also supported. It contains the main
campaign but omits optional Survival, Burst Rifle and ~2x UI data. README.md
records its exact archive identity and the difference from a complete donor.

Do not place the PortMaster release zip here. That zip belongs in PortMaster's
autoinstall folder. See README.md for the complete installation instructions.
